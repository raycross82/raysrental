For a DFW party, a guest count tells you how much furniture you need. Your venue, access and schedule help you decide how to get it there. Use this guide to prepare a clear request so the final quote matches the work you need.

What should you confirm for each option?

  • Customer pickup: You plan the trip to collect and return the rental items. Ask for the confirmed collection location, appointment times and equipment dimensions before choosing a vehicle.
  • Delivery: Ask where the equipment will be unloaded and where it needs to go. Do not assume a delivery quote includes carrying everything upstairs or arranging furniture throughout a venue.
  • Setup: Specify whether you want tables unfolded, chairs arranged and furniture placed according to a layout. Ask Ray to confirm the work and price in the final quote.
  • Collection and teardown: Confirm who folds and stacks the items, where they should be ready, and the agreed collection or return window. A drop-off plan alone does not settle the end-of-event details.

These are planning questions, not a promise that every access or setup request can be accommodated. Ray will confirm the available service for your location and date.

When is customer pickup worth considering?

Pickup may suit you if you have a suitable vehicle, people to help handle the furniture and time for both collection and return. The pickup service itself is free, but you still need to plan your own transport and handling.

Before choosing it, send your exact quantities and ask for folded dimensions. Check the vehicle's usable cargo space, opening size and load limits. Do not rely on guest count or a photo of a vehicle to decide that an order will fit. Keep seating, serving tables, coolers and other cargo in your plan.

If you are still working out quantities, start with the table and chair seating guide and check the current inventory and pricing.

What details help with a delivery quote?

Share the event address and city, plus the actual route from the unloading point to the event area. A driveway beside a backyard and a room reached through a loading dock can require different arrangements. Useful details include:

  • Stairs, elevators, gates, narrow doorways or a long carry from parking.
  • Venue loading rules, a reserved loading window and the contact who can provide access.
  • Where the tables and chairs should be left, and whether you want them arranged.
  • When the space opens, when guests arrive and when the venue must be cleared.
  • An indoor or covered alternative if your outdoor plan changes.

Ask the venue for its current rules directly. Do not assume a rental booking includes permission to use a park, community room or loading area. For workplace events, also see our corporate event rental information.

Common pickup, delivery and setup questions

Does delivery automatically include setup?

Do not assume it does. Ray's Rentals quotes delivery and optional setup separately by location. Describe the arrangement you want and check that the final quote includes it.

Can I request delivery without deciding on setup yet?

You can ask Ray to explain the options when requesting a quote. Confirm the scope before booking so everyone knows who will unfold, arrange, fold and stack the furniture.

Is an event start time the same as a delivery appointment?

No. Provide both your event start time and the venue's access window, then confirm the delivery appointment with Ray. Leave time for the setup you have agreed to; do not treat a preferred time as confirmed availability.

What should I check before the event ends?

Check the agreed return or collection time, who is responsible for teardown, the collection location and who will be available to hand over the equipment. If plans change, contact Ray to confirm revised arrangements.
