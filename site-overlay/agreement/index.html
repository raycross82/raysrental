Rental Agreement

Agreement RR-AGR-v1.0

  1. Parties and Term. This agreement is between Ray's Rentals ("Owner") and the customer named above ("Renter") for the rental period shown above.
  2. Equipment and Charges. Renter rents the equipment itemized above at the rates shown. Charges are per day.
  3. Deposit and Acceptance. Signing this agreement is Renter's request to book. It becomes a confirmed booking only when Owner confirms availability and receives the deposit. Owner will confirm within one business day. A deposit of 25% of the confirmed total is then due to reserve the date. The deposit compensates Owner for holding the date and removing the equipment from availability to other customers, and once paid it is non-refundable.
  4. Balance. The remaining balance is due on delivery, before setup begins.
  5. Cancellation. Once a deposit is paid the date is reserved and the deposit is not refunded if Renter cancels for any reason. If Owner cannot fulfil the request, no deposit is taken and nothing is owed by either party. Renter may cancel by notifying Owner at (469) 571-8720.
  6. Delivery, Setup and Pickup. Renter must provide clear, safe access to the delivery location and ensure someone 18 or older is present at delivery and pickup. If Owner cannot access the location at the agreed time, or no adult is present, Owner may treat the reservation as cancelled and the deposit is forfeited.
  7. Care and Use. Renter will keep equipment on the property at the address above, use it only for its intended purpose, and not drag, stack unevenly, stand on, modify or sublet it. Equipment must be protected from weather when not in use.
  8. Weather. Outdoor events are Renter's risk. Renter is responsible for securing equipment against wind and rain. Weather is not grounds for a refund of the deposit.
  9. Loss and Damage. Renter is responsible for all equipment from delivery until pickup, including loss and theft. Missing or damaged items beyond normal wear are charged at these replacement rates:
    • 6-ft folding table — $60 each
    • Folding chair — $15 each
    • Drink cooler — $25 each
    • JBL PartyBox 110 speaker — $400 each
  10. Condition on Return. Equipment must be returned reasonably clean and stacked where Owner delivered it. Items soiled beyond ordinary cleaning that cannot be restored are charged at the replacement rates above.
  11. Assumption of Risk and Indemnity. Renter assumes responsibility for the safe use of the equipment during the rental period and agrees to hold Owner harmless from claims arising out of Renter's use, misuse, or the conduct of Renter's guests, except to the extent caused by Owner's own negligence.
  12. Unsafe Conditions. Owner may decline to set up or may remove equipment if conditions at the site are unsafe. If Owner does so for safety reasons, Renter is charged only for the deposit already paid.
  13. Electronic Signature. Renter agrees to sign this agreement electronically and that an electronic signature has the same legal effect as a handwritten one.
  14. Governing Law. This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
