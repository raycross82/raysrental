What goes under your tent?

At a backyard party, guests can move between the house and the yard. At a reception, everyone may need a seat at the same time. Build the quote around that moment.

Graduations & open houses
If guests arrive in waves, count the seats you want filled at one time. A buffet and a cooler can have their own covered spot.
Backyard weddings & quinceañeras
List dinner seats, the head table and the cake table separately. Decide whether the DJ and dancing also need cover.
Church gatherings & company events
For a welcome station, start with a narrow tent. For a meal after the program, plan around the number of people who sit together.

How many people fit under a party tent?

Start with the number seated at once and the shapes of your tables. Four 6-ft tables with six chairs each means 24 dining seats before you add a serving table, walkways or a DJ area. The size pages show example layouts to discuss with Ray.
