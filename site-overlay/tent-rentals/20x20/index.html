Tent rental questions

How much larger is a 20x20 than a 10x20?

It adds 200 square feet, doubling the covered area from 200 to 400 square feet. Both are 20 ft long. The extra 10 ft of width is what gives you more ways to arrange dining tables.

What if I have 30 guests and only 20 will sit at once?

Give us both numbers. We can work from the 20 seats you need, then account for standing visitors, food service and the route between the yard and the house.
