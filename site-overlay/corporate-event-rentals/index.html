Questions

Corporate Rental FAQ

How many tables do I need for a classroom-style seminar?
Two people per 6-ft table is the comfortable standard, so plan on one table for every two attendees. A 50-person seminar needs 25 tables. Three per table works if attendees only need a notepad.
Do you deliver and set up?
Yes. Every corporate job is delivered, set up in your layout before your start time, then torn down and picked up after. Delivery and setup start at $150.
How far in advance should I book?
Two weeks is ideal. Shorter timelines are often possible - call and ask, since same-week dates are frequently open.
Do you provide linens?
Not at this time. Tables are bare 6-ft folding tables. If your event needs linens we can point you to a supplier.
Do you have round tables?
No. All tables are 6-ft rectangles. They seat six comfortably for a meal and work well for classroom, expo and boardroom layouts.
What areas do you serve?
All of Dallas-Fort Worth.
