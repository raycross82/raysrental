Party & Event Rentals in Cedar Hill, TX

Most of what leaves our lot headed for Cedar Hill is furniture, not entertainment. A graduation party needs somewhere for forty people to sit down and eat at once. A quinceañera needs a head table and seating pushed to the edges so there is room to dance. A family reunion needs long rows in the shade that stay up from lunch until dark. Inflatables and tents are somebody else's specialty. Tables and chairs are ours, which is why every order gets counted twice and stacked in the order you will unload it.

That covers just about everything we deliver in Cedar Hill: backyard birthdays, graduations, quinceañeras, church and community gatherings, and family reunions. The add-ons are simple. A $12 drink cooler keeps a crowd out of your kitchen fridge, and the $30 JBL PartyBox handles a toast, a first dance, or a playlist that has to carry across a yard.

If you are not sure how many tables a headcount actually needs, our seating guide does the math for you before you commit to a number. The short version: guests spread out more than you expect, and nobody has ever complained about two spare chairs.

Renting a Cedar Hill Park Pavilion? Start Two Weeks Out

Cedar Hill has 22 city parks across 591 acres, and 15 covered pavilions can be rented at parks around the city, plus the Amphitheater at Valley Ridge Park. For an outdoor party in summer, a roof you do not have to put up yourself is worth more than almost anything you can rent.

The part that catches people out is the calendar. Pavilion reservations go through the Parks and Recreation office, and they have to be made a minimum of two weeks in advance. There is no such thing as a same-week pavilion here, so the order of operations matters: pick your date, reserve the pavilion first, and only then build the rental around it. Booking tables before you have a confirmed pavilion is how people end up moving a party into a backyard at the last minute.

Two weeks is comfortable notice for us, and we would rather you use it. When you reserve, write down which park, which pavilion, and the exact hours you have it — those three details are all we need to plan a delivery window that puts everything in place before your first guest arrives. A pavilion usually comes with some fixed picnic tables, but rarely enough for the headcount you actually invited. Count what is bolted down, then rent the difference; that is the cheapest order you will ever place with us.

Cedar Hill also draws visitors from well outside the city. Cedar Hill State Park is the second most visited state park in Texas, with almost 2 million visitors a year, and Joe Pool Lake is right there. If your event is anywhere near either one on a warm Saturday, give yourself extra room in the schedule for the load-in rather than cutting it close to your start time.

When the weather or the season pushes you indoors, the Alan E. Sims Cedar Hill Recreation Center has 5,400 square feet of space used for weddings, receptions and birthday parties. Check what furniture comes with the room before you order from us — we would much rather deliver the twenty tables you need than the thirty you thought you needed.

Cedar Hill FAQs

Good to Know

How much does it cost to rent tables and chairs in Cedar Hill?
Six-foot folding tables are $8 a day and folding chairs are $2 a day. Drink coolers are $12 and a JBL PartyBox speaker is $30. Pickup at our DFW lot is free; delivery to Cedar Hill is priced by your location.
Can you deliver tables and chairs to a Cedar Hill park pavilion?
Yes. You reserve the pavilion through the Cedar Hill Parks and Recreation office at least two weeks in advance, then tell us which park and what time you have it. We deliver the tables and chairs to the pavilion and pick them up after.
How far ahead should I book my Cedar Hill rental?
A 25% deposit reserves your date, and spring and summer Saturdays fill first. If you're booking a city pavilion two weeks out, reserve your tables and chairs in the same sitting so the two dates can't drift apart.
